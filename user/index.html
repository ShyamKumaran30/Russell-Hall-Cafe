About RH Brunch

Russell Hall Café is your neighbourhood spot for proper brunch, golden toasties, and gourmet burgers — all in a warm, chalkboard-chic setting inspired by the best cosy clubs.

Whether you're grabbing a breakfast muffin on the go, settling in for a full English, or enjoying a signature coffee with friends, every visit feels like a little celebration.
